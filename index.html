Meet the Visionary

Muskan Tanted

Professional Choreographer | Anchor | Dancer | Engineer

Muskan Tanted beautifully bridges technical precision with boundless creative energy. As the founder of Muskan Dance Studio, she has created a vibrant artistic haven in the heart of Indore where rhythmic expression thrives.

Whether she's crafting easy-to-learn viral choreographies for festivals like Janmashtami, pacing high-intensity Zumba steps, or working with domestic and international students, her core mission is simple: making dance welcoming, joyful, and deeply empowering for all age groups.
